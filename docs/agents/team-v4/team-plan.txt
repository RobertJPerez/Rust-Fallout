RUST FALLOUT: TEAM V4 — PREPARED, NOT ACTIVATED

Outcome first
Build a connected NV route: boot a source-backed scene; move a canonical player
against authored collision; activate an object; change inventory or quest state;
show the result; Save; exit; Continue in a fresh process with the same state.
Then extend normal-input dialogue, actor behavior and door traversal. Decoders,
inspection commands and test counts alone do not demonstrate a playable game.
Full NV/DLC parity comes before expanding the same engine to 3/4/76 profiles.

Why change the organization
Seven producer queues were converging on one coordinator, with repeated mapping,
proof setup and tiny handoffs. There is substantial existing parser, save, actor,
resource and rendering work. The priority is to connect and validate it, not
replace it with another importer, VM or save format. The integration candidate
contains work newer than the last combined proof; those states must stay distinct.

Staffing
Three technical leads: coordinator, gameplay lead, engine lead. Leads write shared
application/scene adapters and resolve real integration failures. Ten production
and acceptance workers: scripts, runtime, actors, assets, world, presentation,
physics, audio, behavior acceptance, engine acceptance. There is no paperwork-only
backlog worker. Sol is recommended for leads and Luna for bounded worker packages;
this is an allocation recommendation, not a subscription-usage or speed guarantee.
Escalate a difficult semantic investigation to a lead rather than silently guess.

Two integration streams
Gameplay lead integrates scripts/runtime/actors. Engine lead integrates assets/
world/presentation/physics/audio. Each lead works only in its own staging tree.
Acceptance owners produce executable independent tests and frozen-route evidence.
Coordinator receives two coherent tested subsystem batches, fixes shared identity,
source-store/platform problems, owns original-profile infrastructure and main.
Cross-stream dependencies publish exact development commits promptly; they do not
wait for a checkpoint announcement. Root still proves combined compatibility.

Package size and completion
Aim for a complete consumer capability in roughly 2-5 coherent commits, rather
than one handoff per helper. Do not force unrelated changes into a large batch.
A package names actual consumer, source inputs, acceptance and refusals; producer
work that lacks its consumer is explicitly partial. One receipt per package holds
base/head/dependencies, changed paths, commands/results and evidence hashes.
Validate changed behavior once on its actual source, then affected combined paths;
do not rerun every historical immutable proof for every micro-commit.

Dependency readiness is separate from feature acceptance
A producer may publish a tested, named API slice for its consumer while the
larger feature remains open. For example, validated animation samples can unblock
the renderer; the animation feature is accepted only after visible consumption.
Do not wait for a renderer to exist before releasing its producer dependency,
or claim the producer feature complete from its unit tests. Update the same
package receipt with dependency-ready and accepted evidence; no per-helper grant
or extra handoff is required. Proof and promotion dependencies always require
completed acceptance evidence, not just a harness or candidate API.

Continuous useful work
Each lane has eight concrete packages and an owned fallback list. These 104
packages are an initial queue, not an estimate of all work remaining across the
engine and four games. Select a ready package without a grant. At three remaining
actionable packages, append at least
three meaningful owned follow-ons in your outbox with consumer/exit/dependencies;
the lead incorporates them at the next task boundary. Do not wait for that copy.
Handoff publication does not end a worker's run. Continue the next ready package.
At two outstanding packages, the lead prioritizes review before starting another

rather than growing an unlimited dependent branch. Do not busy-poll or invent
metrics-only work to look active. No process design guarantees infinite service
availability; preserve resumable state on a real session or resource limit.

Fallbacks while a build, producer or review is unavailable
Read relevant source completely; implement independent owned module behavior;
write independent fixture expectations; investigate a reproduced route failure;
fix robustness/performance in an existing owned consumer; finish current draft
tests; prepare a concrete additive interface proposal. Do not repeat completed
audits, fabricate a successful stub or write into another owner's checkout.

Resource limits

with jobs 1 initially. More chats do not add RAM, disk or GPU capacity. Use the
central tested wrapper, private targets and immutable proof inputs. Busy exit 75

frozen proof operations so leads keep integrating/coding. No wrapper bypass.
