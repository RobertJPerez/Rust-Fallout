# Exact FNV CELL owner and rank source inputs

`fallout cell-ownership-sources --install <root> --load-order <ordered.json> --cell <Plugin.esm:hex>` consumes the exact owner/rank request. Optional `--index-cache <private-cache>` reuses the existing strict header index. Reports distinguish declaration presence, owner resolution and winning header availability, while ownership/access/theft/actor-faction rank truth remain unevaluated. Exit zero means an exact prepared source closure, including absent, rank-only and explicitly unavailable inputs. Factory refusals emit a null source receipt and error with exit one; earlier strict indexing failures return the original error without manufacturing JSON authority.

`world::ownership::CellOwnershipSources::load(&mut RecordStore, &FormKey, Limits)` seals one exact winning, live NV CELL and its complete ordered source cohort. A private immutable Arc prevents serialized reports from constructing authority. Borrowed `receipt`, `root`, `identity` and `validate_sources` provide inspection and strict reuse checks.

Optional XOWN retains its exact four-byte raw word, canonical NPC_/FACT dependency status and actual winning target header/source digest. Optional XRNK independently retains its raw word and exact signed32 value, including negative and extreme values. Source order, physical and visitor field ordinals, decoded header/payload spans and complete raw framing remain exact. Validated XXXX framing is retained; compressed records never claim a physical decoded-field offset. Absent owner or rank, rank without owner, null words, missing/deleted/wrong-kind winners remain explicit unknown. No absence or zero value means unowned, and no actor/faction rank, theft, access, inventory or ownership truth is computed. Target bodies remain unread.

The existing strict Store, subrecord visitor, CELL decoder, canonical resolver and source receipts are reused. Duplicates, short fields, FO4's wider owner layout, malformed frames, missing masters, tainted CELL bodies, wrong/deleted roots and changed cohorts refuse the request. Unexpected XGLB refuses the NV ownership branch as raw unknown/unsupported with its original decoded header and byte extent, without promoting a TES4 GLOB dependency or leaking partial owner authority. Unknown unrelated fields remain in the CELL body digest.

Eight lowerable ceilings precharge before retained copies and resolution: 256 sources, two record witnesses, 65536 physical fields, one owner link, 4 MiB record bytes, 4 MiB read bytes, 256 raw framing bytes and 16 MiB copied/key metadata. Exact/one-under bounds, stable retry identity, independent FACT/NPC_/signed-rank literals, master remapping, compressed and XXXX spans, strict taint and full-cohort reuse are tested.
